Hardware Testing Made Simple

A centralized platform for performing User Acceptance Testing on company devices before deployment.

SCREEN CHECK

Dead Pixel Test

Click Start to enter full-screen mode. Use the Arrow Keys or Spacebar to cycle through solid colors and inspect the display for dead or stuck pixels. Press Esc to exit.

KEYBOARD CHECK

Keyboard and Trackpad Test

Press each key on your keyboard and use the touchpad to verify that all inputs register correctly. System keys, such as the Windows key, may still trigger operating system functions.

LAPTOP HEALTH CHECK

Battery Health Status

Open Command Prompt and run powercfg /batteryreport to generate a battery report. Open the report and enter the Design Capacity and Full Charge Capacity values below to calculate your battery health.

Results

Note: Browsers cannot read battery health information directly. A Windows battery report is required to obtain the necessary values.

Storage Health Status

Download and open HD Sentinel to perform a storage health scan. Review the drive's Health and Performance status, then record the results in the UAT form.

Download HD Sentinel Here

Note: Browsers cannot access SMART raw health data directly. A dedicated storage diagnostic tool, such as HD Sentinel, is required to evaluate the health and performance of your storage drive.

CAMERA, MIC & SPEAKER CHECK

Camera Test

Select a camera and click Start Camera. Allow browser access and verify that the selected webcam displays a clear and stable video feed.

Camera Preview

Select a camera to begin.

Mic Test

Select a microphone and click Record. Speak into the selected microphone and confirm that the waveform responds to your voice. Stop the recording and play it back to check the audio quality.

Select a microphone to begin.

Speaker Test

Play the test audio through the left, right, or both speakers. Verify that the sound is clear and coming from the selected speaker. Adjust the test volume as needed.
